Form-Associated Elements (Broken)

Custom elements are not form-associated by default. Without static formAssociated = true and this.attachInternals(), the browser doesn't know these elements exist as form fields — their values are invisible to FormData and native validation is bypassed entirely.

Sign in

These fields look like inputs, but the form can't see them.

Submit the form — even with empty fields. Notice that the form submits without complaint and FormData contains no entries for the custom elements. The browser treats them as unknown elements, not form controls.
