Imperatively Creating a Shadow Root

Call attachShadow() on any element at runtime to replace its light-DOM children with an isolated shadow tree. Once attached, the original content is no longer rendered — only what you place in the shadow root is visible.

Click the button to call attachShadow(). The original quote will disappear and be replaced by new content written directly into the shadow root.
