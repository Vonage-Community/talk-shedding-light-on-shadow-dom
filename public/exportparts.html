exportparts

::part() only reaches one shadow level deep. When a component nests another component inside its own shadow root, the inner component's parts are hidden from the page. The exportparts attribute forwards inner parts outward under new names so consumers can style them from anywhere in the tree.

Rocinante Active

The Rocinante ("Roci") is a former MCRN Corvette-class light frigate turned independent warship. Originally commissioned as the MCRN Tachi, she was renamed by James Holden after his crew escaped the Donnager aboard her.

exportparts-card declares exportparts="dot: badge-dot, label: badge-label" on the nested <status-badge>. This maps the badge's inner dot and label parts to badge-dot and badge-label — reachable from the page with exportparts-card::part(badge-dot).
