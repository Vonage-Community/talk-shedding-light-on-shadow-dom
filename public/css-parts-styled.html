CSS Parts

Shadow DOM encapsulates styles — page CSS cannot reach inside a custom element's shadow root. Even if you write a perfectly valid rule targeting a component's internals, the browser silently ignores it. The ::part() pseudo-element is the opt-in escape hatch, but only works when the component author explicitly marks elements with a part attribute.

Donnager

The MCRN Donnager was the flagship of the Martian Congressional Republic Navy's Jupiter fleet and the namesake of the Donnager-class battleship. Almost 500 meters long and weighing approximately 250,000 tons, she dwarfed her United Nations Navy counterparts.

Read more

Like most MCRN ships, she was sleek and angular, covered in thick armor plating — shaped like a long broadhead arrow tip, with four engine nacelles fanning out like the fletching of an arrow. Each nacelle housed an RT-10 'Kaminari' Epstein Drive.

Halfway down her frame were two enormous doors leading into her expansive internal hangar. Positioned forward were two VX-12 "Foehammer" ultra-heavy railgun turrets, each almost twice as long as a Corvette-class frigate, supported by 59 Nariman Dynamics 40mm PDCs.

Rocinante

The Rocinante ("Roci") is a former MCRN Corvette-class light frigate turned independent warship. Originally commissioned as the MCRN Tachi, she was renamed by James Holden after his crew escaped the Donnager aboard her.

Read more

Akin to a narrow, tall building, the ship's construction is layered floor-by-floor from the drive cone and reactor upwards to the flight deck and sensor relays. Under thrust, g-forces provide a downward pull simulating gravity.

As a Corvette-class frigate the ship was capable of housing thirty Martian officers and crew. Her normal independent crew complement was four, with the occasional addition of passengers or marines.

Nauvoo

The LDSS Nauvoo, later known as OPAS Behemoth and then Medina Station, is a generation ship constructed at Tycho Station. Cylindrical in shape, it measured just over two kilometers long and half a kilometer wide. Four Donnager-class battleships would fit in her belly and not touch the walls.

Read more

It was intended to be populated by thousands of Mormons and travel beyond the Sol system to Tau Ceti. However, before its voyage, the ship was used by members of the Outer Planets Alliance to attempt to divert the course of Eros during the Eros incident. The attempt failed and the ship was salvaged by Drummer, rechristened as the OPAS Behemoth. It later underwent refurbishment to transform it from a generational travel ship, into a functional battleship that would be on par with those of the Martian Congressional Republic Navy and United Nations Navy.
